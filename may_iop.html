MILP = the overloads the optimisation expects after its RAs (linear model); the final AC check computes them with a full AC load flow. The limit of a scanned element is its flow before any RA, so it cannot be overloaded before the RAs. The RAs bring the one overloaded RTE branch from 104.30 % to 98.27 % (948.13 A to 893.28 A, limit 909.00 A) in all 3 contingencies. Relieving it shifts flow onto one scanned branch: the MILP accepts 27.41 A of scanned overload there, and the AC check finds 7 scanned pairs 1.2 to 6.0 A over their limit.

Data observations

  • Equipment references: Some contingencies reference equipment not available or not supported in the CGM, particularly three-winding transformers. PST actions also show inconsistent equipment references, although some can be resolved by the parser.
  • Remedial action availability: No curative remedial actions are available in the provided data. Countertrade availability is also very limited, with only one TSO providing one upward and one downward bid.
  • TATL limits: Most secured branches have no TATL in the CGM. For about 93 % of them, the prototype uses the fallback procedure: the TATL is the PATL multiplied by a configurable coefficient, with a configured duration.
